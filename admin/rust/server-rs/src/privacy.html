Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Overview

Soyeht is a terminal client for iOS that connects to your self-hosted Linux servers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data We Do Not Collect

Soyeht does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no cookies. All connections are made directly between your device and your own server.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and never sent to us:

  • Authentication token (stored securely in iOS Keychain, expires after 30 days)
  • Server host address
  • App preferences (theme, font size, cursor style, haptic settings)
  • Custom pane tab nicknames

Camera Access

The app requests camera access solely to scan QR codes for server authentication. No photos or video are captured, stored, or transmitted.

Microphone Access

The app offers optional voice input using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition. Audio is processed locally and is not sent to Soyeht or any third party.

Network Connections

All network connections (REST API and WebSocket) are made directly between your device and your self-hosted server over TLS. No data passes through Soyeht infrastructure.
