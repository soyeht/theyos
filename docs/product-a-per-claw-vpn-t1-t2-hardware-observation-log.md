## Observations (each claim → its authoritative evidence)

### Datapath establishment — runner self-report markers
On datapath start the runner reported: `session_ack_ok=true`, `ack_mtu=1280`,
`session_id_present=true`, `mesh_ipv6_present=true`, `tun_opened=true`,
`route_installed=true`, `packet_pump_started=true`. These are the runner's own
"datapath started" markers. They are corroborated as *real* by the traffic result
below — packets could not have flowed unless the session, interface, and route were
actually up.

### Sustained bidirectional forwarding — AUTHORITATIVE: injected traffic
ICMP was injected toward the claw `/32` (`198.18.0.2`) through the tunnel interface,
in two bursts:

| Burst | Sent | Received | Loss | RTT (avg) |
|-------|------|----------|------|-----------|
| 1     | 40   | 40       | 0%   | ~12.4 ms  |
| 2     | 20   | 20       | 0%   | ~11.7 ms  |
| **Total** | **60** | **60** | **0%** | — |

ICMP echo requires **both directions** to carry, so 60/60 with 0% loss is end-to-end
bidirectional delivery — sustained over the traffic window, measured, no loss, no
stall. This is the authoritative proof of sustained forwarding on hardware.

### No idle-stall — AUTHORITATIVE: the 2026-07-07 comparison
A separate no-traffic run stopped cleanly on `idle_budget_exhausted` (all counters 0).
On 2026-07-07, on this same box, the old blocking pump **stalled** on the idle
direction. The pollable pump now detects idle and stops on a bounded budget instead of
hanging — the 2026-07-07 finding, observed fixed on the same hardware.

### Route reachability + teardown — AUTHORITATIVE: before/after sweeps
- The claw `/32` route was installed and **reachable** (the pings above prove
  reachability into the tunnel). *Scope note:* only claw-`/32` reachability was
  verified; a complete route-scope negative (e.g. default route provably unchanged)
  was not separately captured.
- After each run, before/after sweeps showed **no leftover datapath processes, no
  doc-range routes, and no leftover tunnel interfaces**. The runner self-cleans its
  route + interface on a clean stop; runs that were stopped manually cleaned via
  fd-close (interface auto-destroy).

## Honesty caveat — why forwarding is attributed to ping, not the runner counter

The runner's internal `forwarded=N` counter prints **only** on a clean
`idle_budget_exhausted` stop. Under continuous traffic the pump stays busy (never
idles), and forcing a stop via relay-EOF is an error-exit that does not print the
clean counter line. So the traffic runs' forwarding is evidenced by **ping delivery
(60/60, 0% loss, measured RTT)** — end-to-end and authoritative — rather than by the
runner's internal counter. This is deliberate: authoritative delivery evidence over an
optimistic internal line.
